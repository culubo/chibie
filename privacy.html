Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Who we are

chibie is a small, home-based microgreens grower near Boston, MA. Questions about this policy or your information: sealu@culubo.org.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number (if you give it), pickup or delivery details, and what you ordered.
  • Payment: card payments are handled by a third-party payment processor (such as Stripe or Square). Your card number goes directly to them; we never see or store it. We receive confirmation of payment, the amount, and the last four digits of your card.
  • Survey: your answers, plus your neighborhood or school and email if you choose to give them.
  • Stock updates: if you sign up on the site or in the survey, your email. We use it only to tell you what's growing and when it's ready. Every email has a way to unsubscribe, or just email us.
  • Visiting the site: we use cookie-free analytics that count page views and general information like browser type and country. It doesn't identify you or track you across other sites. Our web host may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) for security.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To keep the records we need for taxes and accounting.
  • To understand which pages are useful, using the anonymous analytics above.

We don't send marketing emails unless you ask to hear from us, and we never sell or rent your information.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the shop: our payment processor, our web host, and our email provider. Each only gets what it needs to do its job. We'll also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The checkout may set essential cookies from our payment processor to process payments and prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for tax and accounting, which is usually a few years, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, unless we're required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you by emailing sealu@culubo.org. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep your information with trusted services and limit who can access it. No system is perfectly secure, but we don't store payment card details at all.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
