What are microgreens?

Young vegetable and herb plants, cut 1–3½ weeks after planting, once their first leaves open. Unlike sprouts, they grow in a growing medium and are cut above the root, so you eat just the stem and leaves.

4–40×

By weight, microgreens can hold 4–40 times the vitamins of the grown plant.

USDA study, 2012

7%

Only 7% of adults 18–30 eat enough vegetables. A handful on your sandwich, ramen or eggs helps.

CDC, 2019

Hi, I'm the grower

I'm a senior studying computer science at Boston University, growing microgreens on the side. I care about eating well in a way that's about more than macros: food that's fresh, colorful and actually tastes good.

chibie is my small experiment in making that easy for students and neighbors around Boston. If you're curious, want to help grow, or just want to see how it works, I'd love to hear from you in the survey or at sealu@culubo.org.
